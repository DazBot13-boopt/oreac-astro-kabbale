<<<KEY planeteSigne.j|Vierge Pluton en Vierge>>>
1957/8 -- 1971
Individuals from the Pluto in Virgo generation need to transform their nervous energies of worry and insecurity by being of service to people and nature. This generation has already done much to reduce the exploitation of workers and eliminate servants. Virgo is a sign that is associated with health, work, and the practical application of natural sciences resulting in new technologies. The transition of Pluto through Virgo marked an era characterized by revolutionary changes in industrial relations, production methods, and medicine. Computers gave new life to science, trade, and industry. Trade unions emerged as powerful political agitators, and the packaging and preservation of foodstuffs was introduced. The use of automation in the production of industrial goods made much of the drudgery of human manpower superfluous. There were great medical advancements due to the many exciting medical discoveries made. The use of hallucinogenic drugs in the early sixties introduced new, but uncontrolled, levels of consciousness, which led to a social revolution amongst the young.

Pluto is assigned to Scorpio, the sign of sexuality, and Virgo is the sign of health, so this period saw the introduction of new methods of contraception and the development of new forms of sexual ethics. A dramatic increase in the chemical pollution of foodstuffs became a major problem and led to a new movement supporting natural and organically produced foodstuffs.
